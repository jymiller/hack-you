Opsera's DevSecOps agent gates this repository. A pre-commit hook runs five scanners over the whole tree and blocks the commit if the staged lines introduce a Critical or High finding. Pre-existing findings warn but don't block — so the gate stops new risk without freezing the work. Everything below is read from the scan report committed next to the code.
