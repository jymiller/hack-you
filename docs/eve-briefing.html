What we're building — "Covenant Sentinel"

A real-time agent that watches the live web for events threatening a borrower's loan covenant, does cited deep research via You.com's Research API, recomputes the covenant on your own books, and routes a breach to a human analyst to sign off. The persona pitch: "an analyst covers 40 borrowers and can't read every filing — Sentinel catches the one covenant that silently breached, with citations, in under a minute."

The money-shot (the whole demo hinges on this): a live ARI crawl surfaces fresh news that sponsor Ardenmoor restated its accounts real → the kernel recomputes borrower Thornwick from a certified 6.47× GREEN to 7.59× BREACH on synthetic books synthetic → a cited research package routes to the analyst → after sign-off, a notice is served prerun. One truth on screen: the certificate reads GREEN, the recompute says BREACH.

The ARI latency fix (already solved)

ARI takes 13.3s — fatal dead air in a 180s demo if called synchronously. The fix, verified in the docs tonight: {"background": true} returns instantly with a task_id + stream_url; fire it at the first slide, narrate the persona while it runs, and pull the finished cited result ~15s later exactly when you need it on screen. The demo script (generating now) is built around this beat.
