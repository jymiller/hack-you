# PITCH LANGUAGE — Covenant Sentinel

The words we say on stage and put on the page. Derived from a 5-voice pitch tournament (showman / security-skeptic /
credit-expert / plain-English / Wet-Ink) — the lines below are where all five converged.

**Status: live document.** John is still refining. Update this file when the language changes, and keep
`web/index.html` in agreement with it.

---

## The frame (this is the whole thing)

Lead with **scale**, not mechanism. The single caught breach is the **proof**, not the story.

> At 40 deals, an analyst can check. At 4,000, nobody can — so "all green" becomes something you *hope*,
> not something you *know*.

## The winning words

| Slot | The line | Why it wins |
|---|---|---|
| **Hook** | *"How do you know all 6,000 are green?"* | A question the buyer can't answer. Opens the wound before the fix. |
| **Contrast** | *"Green because we checked — not because nobody looked."* | The whole value prop in one sentence. |
| **Villain** | *"The silent breach."* | Names the enemy in two words — a breach that never turns red. |
| **Core** | *"Humans don't scale. Recomputation does."* | Why software is the answer, without saying "AI." |
| **Verb / loop** | *"Watch. Recompute. Catch. Escalate."* | Four words a judge repeats back. Each is a real step. |
| **Mechanism** | *"It never trusts the certified number."* | The defensible core as a principle. A credit person nods. |
| **Unlock** | *"The machine reads all 6,000; the human decides on the 3 that flipped."* | Triage inversion — you aim the analyst, not replace her. |
| **Trust** | *"It finds the breach. It won't file it. A human signs."* | Deny-by-default in one breath. Earns the security judges. |
| **Proof** | *"Here's the receipt."* | The Wet-Ink closer that won. |
| **Persona** | *"One analyst, forty borrowers, can't read every filing."* | The human who hurts, in seven words. |
| **Metric** | *"Caught before the quarterly report."* | Timing, not a fake percentage — true and un-gameable. |

## The proof line

> Thornwick certified leverage at **6.47×** — inside the 6.50× covenant, green. A restatement quietly dropped LTM
> EBITDA £34m→£29m; net debt never moved. The real number is **7.59×** — a breach.

## The 30-second pitch (scale-framed)

> "You run a credit book — say four thousand deals, every dashboard green. But 'green' just means the borrower said so.
> One analyst can re-check forty deals a quarter; nobody re-checks four thousand — so most of your book is green because
> no one looked.
>
> Covenant Sentinel is the analyst that scales. It watches the live web and re-underwrites every covenant on every deal,
> continuously — because it never trusts the certified number. Out of the whole book it catches the one that quietly
> breached on a restatement — 6.47 to 7.59 — and holds it for a credit officer to sign.
>
> The machine reads all four thousand; the human decides on the three that flipped. Green because we checked, not
> because nobody looked. Here's the receipt."

## The honesty line (say it out loud — it scores)

> "The crawl is live and the recompute is live; the borrower is synthetic — and every effect on screen says which it is,
> so nothing real fires on stage."

## Taglines (finalists)

1. **Covenant Sentinel — green because we checked, not because nobody looked.**
2. Covenant Sentinel — it recomputes the number nobody rechecks.
3. It finds the breach. A human signs. Here's the receipt.

## DO NOT SAY (the tournament's cut list)

- ❌ Opening on **"6.47 → 7.59"** — that's proof *inside* the story, never the hook.
- ❌ *"we label every pixel"* → overclaim. Say **"every effect is labeled."**
- ❌ *"rings the bell"*, *"before your coffee's cold"* — cute, breaks the plain register.
- ❌ *"checks their homework"* — condescending to expert judges.
- ❌ *"AI-powered / seamless / revolutionary / real-time intelligence"* — the room discounts hype.
- ❌ *"catches breaches"* (plural, general) — it catches **one synthetic breach, live-recomputed**. Keep it true.
- ❌ Implying 6,000 real deals are monitored. The book is **illustrative**; the engine is what scales.
